## 2. Baki 2018
<img width="738" height="414" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b42ef8b4-36ba-4db6-8052-07cdbdbd400c" />

---
---
**Director:** Toshiki Hirano

**Genre:** Martial Arts/Action

**Why I love it:** Baki Hanma's mindset is defined by an obsessive drive to surpass his father, Yujiro Hanma, paired with a relaxed, unassuming personality outside of battle. Unlike traditional heroes motivated by justice or money, Baki fights purely to test his biological limits and achieve absolute strength.

**favorite line:** "I don't care about being the strongest in the world. I just want to be a little bit stronger than my father.".
